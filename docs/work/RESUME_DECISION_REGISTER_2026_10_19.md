## Decision families and what not to confuse

### FDA-05: scientific semantics vs implementation

- **F5-A**: relative to the hidden rival; adversarial self-review identified sign/zero and generator-shape leakage problems. **Not scientifically accepted.**
- **F5-IS**: interval/probabilistic scoring could be preferable only if the *elicited probabilistic object* and properness conditions are specified. An interval around two deterministic laws is **not automatically a proper probabilistic forecast**.
- **F5-D**: conservative refusal-equivalent fallback removes inflated score but does not prove informative discrimination.
- **GS-4**: independent attacker/positive-control author plus external evaluator; **self-challenge ≠ independent validation**.
- Version any changed certified escrow semantics and prereg-pinned evaluator; never mutate or re-score historic v0.2 artifacts silently.

### FDA-06: representability is not benchmark competence

- Expand law-credit cap only for scientifically justified kinds (the R-06 partial execution is not a full lift PASS reproduction).
- Independent baseline applicability must be determined under a frozen comparable budget and qualified *without reading formal hidden outcomes*. PySR grammar coverage alone cannot remove caps. BoTorch experiment selection is a different capability.
- No silent change to `truth_in_searched_family` meaning or historic RunRecord identities.

### O-1: no p-value by label

- Primary scientific comparison: expected **capped** candidate vs strongest applicable comparator under a valid null, with world-set independence assumptions checked after B-LEAK closure.
- Plain sign-flip on asymmetrically capped differences is not justified solely by a zero expected difference; label permutation with recomputed cap demands the stronger exchangeability null and is at most a separately justified analysis.
- Holm requires valid input p-values; strongest-baseline selection, multiple presentations, the intersection-union non-inferiority conditions and repeated submissions need explicit treatment.
- Pilot power/calibration **cannot** be used to tune post-hoc formal thresholds; acceptance criteria before simulation.

### BVB: scientific review is not adoption

- **Reported six audit hashes** and 97-component classes are preserved in [BVB handoff](BVB_RESUME_HANDOFF_2026_10_19.md).
- Original `KEEP 69 / AUDIT 21` vs proposed `KEEP 67 / AUDIT 23` both listed; no automatic owner sign-off.
- `REPLACE 3` is not automatically urgent just because a library exists.
- Baseline arms must be included, independently qualified and frozen **before** formal Genesis measurement if chosen.
